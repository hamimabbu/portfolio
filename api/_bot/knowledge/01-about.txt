Hasibur Rahaman Hamim (Hamim) is a final-year Bachelor of Computer Science student at the University of Wollongong, majoring in cyber security. He graduates in December 2026.
He learns by building a tool and then testing it until it breaks. His security work covers a file upload scanner, an ARP poisoning detector, an Active Directory home lab and a small web app called LayerLens.
He is based in Wollongong, NSW.
