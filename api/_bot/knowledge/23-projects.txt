This site is Hamim's portfolio, and it lists eight projects.
Four are security projects, and each has its own case study page: the capstone upload scanner, the ARP poisoning detector, the Active Directory home lab and LayerLens, a small web app built around a threat model.
Three are team projects from his degree: the AI-powered online retail system, TalentMatch Pro and the loan default prediction.
The eighth, ExpiryTracker, is his own mobile app idea. It is still a design and is not built yet.
LayerLens and the Active Directory home lab are his own projects outside a team. The capstone and the degree projects are team work, and the ARP detector began as a group assignment.
