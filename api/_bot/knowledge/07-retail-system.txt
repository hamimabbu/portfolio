The AI-powered online retail system is a CSCI318 team project (team of three, in progress): four Spring Boot microservices connected by Kafka events, plus an AI shopping assistant his teammates built.
Hamim owns the Product and Inventory services, the stock change events and the low-stock alerts, and shares the testing with Postman and JUnit.
